## Description
*(Gumroad's description field supports rich text — bold the headings, keep the bullets)*

**Stop burning credits guessing.**

Most prompt packs sell you words and hope. This one shows you the photo first.

**The Blondes** is 12 production-tested prompts for creating stunning, natural blonde AI influencers — and beside every single prompt you'll see the **real, untouched output** from up to four different image models. No cherry-picked hero shot with the prompt hidden. You see exactly what you're buying, before you spend a credit.

**What's inside**

- **12 complete prompts**, written out in full — copy, paste, generate. Nothing to add, nothing to assemble.
- **30+ real generated images** shown next to the prompts that made them, each badged with the model that produced it.
- **A 4-model comparison** on the same prompt (ChatGPT GPT Image 2 · Gemini Nano Banana 2 · Grok Imagine 1.5 · Higgsfield Soul 2) so you can see which engine suits your style.
- **A Director's Note on every scene** — which model won, what phrase is load-bearing, and what to change when a render comes out wrong.
- **Fill-in character template** at the end, so the same 12 scenes work with any character you invent — a redhead, a brunette, any age, any build.
- **Negative prompts included** on every scene.
- Formatted for **9:16 stories and 4:5 feed**.

**The 12 scenes**

Golden Morning Coffee · Trocadéro Sunrise · Parisian Balcony (top-down) · Candlelit Dinner · Beach-Club Daybed (4-model showdown) · Amalfi Sea Ladder · Hotel Suite Unboxing · Caught in the Rain · Airport Sprint · Airplane Window · The Tiny Tattoo · Birthday Sparkler

**No reference photo needed.** Every prompt describes a complete character — age, features, hair, skin, tan lines — so you're not dependent on uploading a face. Each model renders its own woman: same prompt, different person, all of them believable.

**Why these look real and most AI images don't**

The pack teaches the four rules baked into every prompt: one honest named light source (never "studio", never "cinematic"), curated environments with imperfection living in the camera instead of the clutter, precisely specified emotions, and one deliberate flaw per face. That's the whole difference.

**Who it's for**

Creators building AI influencer accounts, social media managers, content studios, and anyone who wants photoreal lifestyle images that don't announce themselves as AI.

**Instant download.** 13-page PDF, print-ready A4.

*Created by Bruno Melício, PhD — AI researcher and university lecturer in deep learning.*
