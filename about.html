Why
Braids?

The name contains AI naturally—BRAIDS. But the meaning is larger than the letters.

A braid is something tangible: separate strands intertwined into one deliberate form. Braids applies that same idea to AI—each model contributes what it does best.

Start with a proven template, combine compatible models in one workflow, or choose a single model yourself. The setup changes; the creator remains in control.

Independent by design.

Braids was founded by AI researcher Bruno Melício as an independent creative practice. It is separate from his academic research, with its own purpose: making high-quality generative tools accessible to creators, brands and curious people.
